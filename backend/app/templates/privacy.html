Privacy notice

NatureCheckpoint is a university project. We collect as little data as possible.

What we store

  • Phone check-ins: a random ID in the cookie nc_visitor (valid for one year). No name, email, phone number or location.
  • Card check-ins: a salted hash of the card number, made on the checkpoint. The card number itself never leaves the checkpoint.
  • Per tap: the checkpoint, the time and whether it counted as a stamp.
  • Vouchers: the mock voucher code of the month.

What we do not store

  • No GPS location, no IP address in the database, no tracking or advertising cookies.
  • The checkpoints measure temperature, humidity and light only. No camera, no microphone.

Why

To count stamps for the monthly reward and to show anonymous visit statistics per checkpoint to the park operator.

Deleting your data

Delete the cookie in your browser. Without the ID, the stored check-ins can no longer be linked to you.
